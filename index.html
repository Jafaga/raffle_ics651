BEHIND THE DRAW

The methods, revealed.

Classical: one software pseudorandom value selects an integer from 0 to 7. Simulated quantum: three independent 50/50 software draws model measuring three qubits after Hadamard gates.

Both have ideal odds of 1 in 8 for every outcome. The simulation uses the same software randomness source—it is not a real quantum device or evidence of a quantum advantage.
